# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Flowork community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, insults, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private
information, and conduct inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication
that violate this code. Reports should use the private contact mechanism in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) until a dedicated community contact is published.
